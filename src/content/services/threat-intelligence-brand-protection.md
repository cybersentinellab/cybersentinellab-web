faqs:
- q: What is brand protection in cybersecurity?
  a: Brand protection is the continuous monitoring of the internet for misuse of your organisation's name, logo and identity — such as phishing sites, fake social accounts and leaked data — followed by action to remove it.
- q: What is threat intelligence?
  a: Threat intelligence is information about current and emerging threats that are relevant to your organisation. It helps you detect attacks earlier and decide where to focus your defences.
- q: Can you take down fake websites and social media accounts?
  a: Yes. Cyber Sentinel Lab works with domain registrars, hosting providers and social platforms to report and remove fraudulent sites and accounts that impersonate your brand.
- q: Why monitor the dark web?
  a: Stolen credentials and data are often traded on the dark web before an organisation knows it has been breached. Monitoring gives you time to reset passwords, close gaps and warn affected people.
