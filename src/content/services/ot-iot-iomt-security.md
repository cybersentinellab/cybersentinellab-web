faqs:
- q: What is OT security?
  a: OT (operational technology) security protects the systems that run physical processes, such as industrial control systems, SCADA and building management. It focuses on availability and safety as much as data.
- q: What is IoMT?
  a: IoMT (Internet of Medical Things) refers to connected medical devices such as infusion pumps, imaging systems and patient monitors. They often lack built-in security, making them attractive targets.
- q: Can you secure OT systems without stopping production?
  a: Yes. Cyber Sentinel Lab uses passive discovery and monitoring techniques designed for operational environments, so assessments do not interrupt production or patient care.
- q: Why are IoT devices a security risk?
  a: Many IoT devices ship with default passwords, outdated firmware and no monitoring. Once compromised, they can give attackers a route into the wider network.
