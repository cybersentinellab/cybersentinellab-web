faqs:
- q: What is Zero Trust security?
  a: Zero Trust is a security model that never trusts any user, device or application by default. Every access request is verified based on identity, device health and context, even inside the corporate network.
- q: Why is identity security important?
  a: Most breaches involve stolen or misused credentials. Protecting identities with strong authentication, least-privilege access and monitoring closes the most common route attackers use.
- q: What is privileged access management (PAM)?
  a: PAM secures the accounts with the highest level of access, such as administrators. It limits who can use them, when, and records what they do.
- q: Can Zero Trust be introduced gradually?
  a: Yes. Cyber Sentinel Lab usually starts with the highest-risk users and systems, then extends controls in stages so business operations are not disrupted.
