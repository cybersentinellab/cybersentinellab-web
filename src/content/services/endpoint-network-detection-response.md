faqs:
- q: What is the difference between EDR and NDR?
  a: EDR (endpoint detection and response) watches activity on individual devices such as laptops and servers. NDR (network detection and response) analyses traffic between devices. Together they give visibility that neither provides alone.
- q: Is antivirus enough to protect our business?
  a: Traditional antivirus mainly blocks known malware. Modern attacks use legitimate tools and stolen credentials, so behaviour-based detection such as EDR and NDR is needed to catch them.
