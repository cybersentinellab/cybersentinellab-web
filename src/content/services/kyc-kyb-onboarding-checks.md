- q: What is KYC and KYB?
  a: KYC (know your customer) verifies the identity of individual customers. KYB (know your business) verifies companies, including their registration, ownership and directors. Both are core requirements for regulated businesses.
- q: Who needs onboarding checks?
  a: Banks, exchange houses, payment and fintech companies, and any business with anti-money-laundering obligations or high fraud exposure.
- q: Can checks be automated?
