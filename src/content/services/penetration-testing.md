faqs:
- q: What is penetration testing?
  a: Penetration testing is an authorised, simulated cyberattack on your systems. Cyber Sentinel Lab's testers use the same techniques as real attackers to find exploitable weaknesses, prove their impact safely, and give you a prioritised plan to fix them.
- q: How often should an organisation run a penetration test?
  a: Most organisations test at least once a year and after any major change, such as a new application, a cloud migration or a network redesign. Regulated sectors such as banking and payments often require testing on a fixed schedule.
- q: Will penetration testing disrupt our live systems?
  a: No. Testing is planned with you in advance, follows agreed rules of engagement, and avoids destructive techniques. Sensitive tests can be scheduled outside business hours.
- q: What is the difference between a vulnerability scan and a penetration test?
  a: 'A vulnerability scan uses automated tools to list known weaknesses. A penetration test goes further: skilled testers chain weaknesses together and attempt to exploit them, showing what an attacker could actually achieve.'
