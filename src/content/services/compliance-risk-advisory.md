faqs:
- q: What is GRC in cybersecurity?
  a: GRC stands for governance, risk and compliance. It is how an organisation sets security direction, manages risk and meets legal and regulatory requirements in a structured, measurable way.
- q: What is UAE IA compliance?
  a: The UAE Information Assurance Regulation sets security controls for government entities and critical-sector organisations in the UAE. Cyber Sentinel Lab assesses where you stand against it and helps you implement the required controls.
- q: How long does ISO 27001 certification take?
  a: For most organisations, ISO 27001 preparation takes several months, depending on size, scope and current maturity. A gap analysis gives a realistic timeline at the start.
- q: Which frameworks do you support?
  a: Cyber Sentinel Lab supports the UAE Information Assurance Regulation (UAE IA), ISO 27001/27002, the NIST Cybersecurity Framework, PCI DSS, GDPR, HIPAA, SOX, FISMA and other industry-specific programmes.
- q: Is compliance the same as security?
  a: No. Compliance shows you meet a defined standard; security is about actually reducing risk. A good GRC programme achieves both, so certification reflects real protection.
