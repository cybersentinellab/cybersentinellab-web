Cyber Sentinel Lab LLC FZ ("Cyber Sentinel Lab", "we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal data we collect through cybersentinellab.com, why we collect it and how we protect it.

## Who we are

Cyber Sentinel Lab LLC FZ, Meydan Road, Nad Al Sheba, Dubai, United Arab Emirates. Contact: [info@cybersentinellab.com](mailto:info@cybersentinellab.com).

## What we collect

- **Information you give us** — your name, email address, company, phone number, the service you are interested in and your message when you use our contact form, email us or message us on WhatsApp.
- **Basic technical data** — we use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which measures page views and performance without cookies and without tracking you across websites.

We do not use advertising cookies and we do not sell personal data.

## How we use it

- To respond to your enquiry and provide the services you request.
- To keep records of our communications with you.
- To protect this website against spam and abuse (Cloudflare Turnstile).
- To meet our legal and regulatory obligations.

## Legal basis

We process your data with your consent (for example, when you submit the contact form), to take steps at your request before entering into a contract, and for our legitimate interests in running and securing our business. Where the UAE Personal Data Protection Law (Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021) or the EU General Data Protection Regulation applies, we process data in line with those laws.

## Sharing

We share data only with service providers who help us operate — such as our website host and email provider — under appropriate confidentiality and security terms, or where required by law. Our group offices in Denmark and India may access enquiries to respond to you.

## Retention

We keep enquiry data for as long as needed to respond and maintain business records, then delete or anonymise it.

## Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, or withdraw your consent, by emailing [info@cybersentinellab.com](mailto:info@cybersentinellab.com).

## Security

As a cybersecurity company, we apply appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data against unauthorised access, loss or misuse.

## Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it was last revised.
